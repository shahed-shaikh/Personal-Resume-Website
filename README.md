ADRIAN VALE is a personal resume website built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and custom animations. Its style combines warm neutrals, burgundy, gold, and serif typography.

The hero introduces Adrian as a Creative Director and Brand Strategist, with a portrait, location, highlights, and links to experience and contact sections. The profile shares a personal statement and creative philosophy.

The career section presents three roles, from Visual Designer to Senior Brand Designer and Creative Director, with dates, organizations and responsibilities. Education highlights a degree in Visual Communication and a foundation in Art & Design.

The expertise section covers Creative Leadership, Brand Development, and Visual Communication. Core skills appear as percentage bars, alongside software tools and language proficiency. A contact section provides an email link and social destinations, followed by a footer.

Built with Tailwind CSS, responsive grids, hover transitions, image scaling, reveal animations, and reduced-motion support, the page presents professional experience in a polished format.
